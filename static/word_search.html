📖 How to Play Word Search Arena

🔍 1. Selecting Words: Click/Touch on the first letter of a word and drag your finger or mouse across to the last letter in any of the 8 directions (horizontal, vertical, diagonal, forward or backwards).
🎨 2. Vibrant Highlights: Found words get highlighted in colorful candy capsules and struck through in your word bank.
👥 3. Two-Player Turn Duel: Players take turns with a 30-second timer. Finding a word scores points based on length + remaining turn time and stamps the word in your signature color (Player 1 Blue, Player 2 Green). Highest score wins!
💡 4. Hints: In 1-Player mode, you have 3 hints available to locate starting letters.
